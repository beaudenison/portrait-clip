# Changelog

## 1.0.1 — 2026-10-07

- Default layout puts the camera bar on top and the gameplay underneath.
- Locked layers keep their shape. Resizing the camera zooms the picture instead of stretching it. You can still unlock a layer.
- Drag the timeline and the source and preview follow the playhead.
- Compile saves only the video. No thumbnail image is written next to the export.
- New look: dark chrome, cream controls, and the footage stays full brightness.
- The bottom-left crop handle is back.
- Built-in layout buttons are gone. Layouts you save yourself still work.
- New app icon, the same portrait mark as the window.
- To update, run the 1.0.1 setup. It replaces 1.0.0. Your clips, layouts, and settings stay on this PC.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-08-19

First public release.

- Import local recordings (MP4, MOV, MKV, WEBM, AVI)
- Crop gameplay and webcam into portrait, square, or landscape
- Preset layouts (Portrait Split, Portrait Crop, Full Blur) plus saved custom layouts
- CapCut-style timeline: split at the playhead, trim pieces, delete pieces
- Local FFmpeg compile at 720p or 1080p, 30 or 60 fps (GPU encode when available)
- Settings for default resolution, FPS, and export folder
- Montages with cut, fade, swipe, or squeeze
- Windows installer; running a newer setup replaces this version
